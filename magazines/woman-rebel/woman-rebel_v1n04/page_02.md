# NEO-MALTHUSIANISM — WHAT IT IS

CLARA NEWCOME

Malthus was an English clergyman who in 1796 published a paraphrase in which he propounded the theory that the human race increased faster than the earth's productiveness to support it. He pointed out that the population increased in geometrical progression as compared to the means of production in arithmetical progression and in order to avoid a final calamity he advocated late marriages, and he also pointed out the necessity of war and pestilence to get rid of the over-production in population.

The upper-class did not object to Malthus' theory, on the contrary they hailed it in all its crude barbarism. They had no objection to the workers increasing in a greater ratio than the means of production, and to make cannon food out of them in war. Of course, pestilence is worse because it knows no class distinction, that was why science was allowed to work to do away with pestilence, while poverty and war remain as the glorified twins that cannot be spared from their work in the race. Social science could so easily do away with poverty and war as medical science did away with pestilence.

The French working-class was the first to practise it. They learned it from the upper class, and from the French workers it spread to the workers in Germany, Belgium and the Scandinavian countries, where they have formed an international society for humanitarian child-bearing, that has for its object to spread the information among the workers, and to help them to get the preventive means. The school of thought that advocates it is called Neo-Malthusian and it counts among its members some of the best thinkers of the times, such as Harald Hoeffding, the Danish philosopher, Knut Wicksell, the Swedish sociologist, in the University of Lund, and many others that dare to come out and express their convictions, in defiance of existing laws.

About thirty years ago Charles Bradlaugh in England was denied a seat in the house of Commons because he believed in Neo-Malthusianism and advocated it together with Mrs. Besant, who wrote a book giving the information to the public. We are inclined to believe that the various laws made against the control of child-bearing in different countries will be about as effective as the laws made against working-class organization or the witchery of old. ## Spread of the Propaganda

In France the ruling-class have appealed to the working woman to bring more children into the world for the love of her country, while they have sent agents into Spain and Italy and all the surrounding countries where the workers still breed like animals to cover dear France with a flood of immigrants to beat down the native worker in the labor market. The French workers have in turn begun to send teachers into the neighboring countries, realizing that the workers have no country and that the world's working-class is a nation by itself that must stand or fall together. Neo-Malthusianism must come into a world-wide practise among the workers and it must create a host of advocates and a literature worthy of its cause. It has already made a fair start in several countries and should have an immense field in America, where thought along new lines is an every-day phenomena.

## Upper Class Have Secret

As we have already hinted, the control of child-bearing in the upper class is pretty much an open secret all over the world. The miserable gush that is passed along the line to the effect that the upper-class woman uses her brain so much that she loses her reproductive power doesn't pass muster among intelligent people. The upper-class women (with the exception of the top layer who become too "sporty" and degenerate to reproduce themselves on a large scale) are on the whole just as prolific as the working women. So that if the upper-class families were not cut down by conscious regulation, they would be as large as the workers' families. They would have families of children to the number of from five to ten.

But as long as the practise of the control of child-bearing was confined to the upper-class, no laws were instituted against it. On the contrary the upper-class men were complimented for their consideration toward their wives in this matter, and the upper-class mother was complimented for her thoughtfulness in not bringing any more children into the world than could receive the best of education and advantage.

When the French workers first began to practise the control of child-bearing, there was a howl made against it. But

after a careful investigation by the state, the learned men came to the conclusion that there was no real social danger in its practice, that at best it could only help the individual worker. This silenced the capitalist moralist, until the factory owners felt the increasing difficulty in getting children and women as workers, and that this kind of cheap labor actually threatened to disappear from the market. They now immediately called upon the state to set all its machinery in motion to compel the workers to reproduce themselves upon a larger scale.

Now began a veritable torrent of drivel from the pulpit and press. The editor that practised Neo-Malthusianism in his own family went into costasy over large families, and stopped all over in his editorial page about the honor due to the parents with large families. The priest whose religion forbade him to have any children commanded the parents in his congregation to multiply and become fruitful according to the will of God. Laws were instituted to put the advocates in jail and to confiscate the literature, with the net result that the worker who hadn't heard of it before became wise. The peasant listening and reading all this, said to his wife:—

"Marie, all they say again it may be true, but nohow I ain't seein' how you and me can afford to have any more children."

## Master-Class Arrangement

Sex relationship is about as private a matter as anything can be and for the capitalists to presume that through the state they can force the workers to multiply is nothing more nor less than a piece of insane arrogance. Let the upper-class show the immorality of the control of child-bearing by refusing to practise it themselves. When they produce on the average families of five to ten children we shall bc'ieve in their sincerity. Let the priests and moralists get to work among the so-called educated class that can really afford to have large families. For a quarter of a century the practice has been general in the upper-class, without one word of protest from the capitalist moralist. When he weeps copious tears about it now and shouts race-suicide, we know that his incentive comes either from the stinking cesspool of rotten conventionality that regards any barbarism as sacred because it is old, or it is a plain matter of pocket-book interest, of position and temporary honor.
