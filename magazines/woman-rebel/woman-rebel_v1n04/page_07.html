When the torch and the hatchet laid

The true meaning of the word "marriage," however much sentimentalism may have perverted that word, was put into language from the beginning of the slavery of wifehood, when man first seized "his woman" and kept her from other men. This meaning is practically a binding to himself on the part of a man, of one woman or of two or more women, for life or for a definite period of time.

Except in rare cases, marriage, in the whole history of the race, has been a contract between man and man, or between a man and himself—never a contract between a woman and a man. We may say that modern civilization has changed all this, but it has not—marriage is still, except in the rarest cases, a contract between man and man. Being a contract made by man for man, woman in this contract is always a slave and never an equal partner to the contract.

The forms of marriage instituted by the social order are Monogamy (so-called), Polygamy and Polyandry. Monogamy has never been anything but a name, such relation never having existed except in hypocritical pretense,

But the militants, strong in the equity of their cause and the historical justification for their methods, are out to win. Imprisonment, forcible feeding and persecution cannot crush the indomitable spirit of the womanhood which has arisen to inflame the world with a new passion for liberty. With its battle scarred heroines ennobled by a high idealism militancy is stronger today than ever before.

The monarchy, the church and the selfish millions who would have woman remain a source of sexual excitation and a kitchen slave all now are combined in one towering mass to crush that organization which has behind it a decade of unparalleled activity, exuberant heroism and an awakened womanhood. In the fight that is now being waged by women against the domination of man, the torch and the hatchet may be followed by the bomb and the bullet. All these weapons will be justifiable for all may be needed in effacing forever the white-livered monster that fattens upon child labor, that hounds women into filthy dens, into factories and prostitution and that denies to her the right of her body and of civic and economic equality.

In the pretense called Monogamy, the woman has not only been a slave, but she has been a slave to a lie. ever, is a reactionary institutional development of the true instinct of sex. In this instinct, scientifically considered, there should be no "binding" of any sort—both woman and man should be absolutely free to obey this instinct in accordance with individual initiative, controlled only by the spirit of reciprocity in the other individuality involved.

This does not mean that individuals should not be protected from other by social control, but it does mean that social control should never take the form of the crushing out of spontaneity as to the exercise of any instinct. It should be used only to keep this instinct to its business of furthering the two great principles of life—self-preservation and race-preservation. Marriage in all its forms has done more than any other social institution to repress these two great principles of life, in the very citadel of life itself, the reproduction of the race. Let this institution be anathema to all thinking minds.

FROM A GIRL WORKER

The "Woman Rebel" came to me as a mother comes to its child. The motherly expressions and truthful explanations of a girl's inclination and temptations appealed to me so much that I can find no words eloquent enough to express my gratitude. It is the first paper of its kind and of course the government is trying hard to suppress the "Woman Rebel" as usually happens when a paper of any kind is published which will benefit the working people. While many agitators such as the I. W. W., Anarchists and Socialists are going around trying to educate the people in order to better conditions and while they undoubtedly do excellent work, yet in order to get at the root of the social question we must face the tragedy of large families and teach the workers the evil of bringing these into the world.

The first issue of the "Woman Rebel" was suppressed and now the third issue is also under the ban of the Postmaster. Both these issues were very mild. The ruling class want sailors, soldiers, hangmen, gunmen and prostitutes and they do all they can to obtain dupes for this purpose.

My personal opinion is that we do not begin to realize how very important the question of voluntary motherhood is for improving conditions. It gets right down to the root of our great social evil. We cease to bring too many slaves into the world. It will enable us to bring up our children better and place us on an equal footing with men so that we can join with them in the great struggle upon the success of which the emancipation of the race from wage slavery depends.

Bella Yollia
