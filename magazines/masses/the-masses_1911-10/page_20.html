ART AND SOCIALISM

CORLEA WINTER

The socialistic interpretation of things has grown deep into the heart of society; it grips everyone, great and small alike, and great artists are among the first to feel the influence of its broadening spirit.

Have you studied this picture by Charles Winter?

Have you looked at our frontispiece "The Great Light," by Anton Otto Fischer?

These pictures, painted by two of our comrades, are works of art of which the American Socialist Movement may be proud.

We will send you two beautiful photogravures of these pictures, 12 inches deep and 10 inches wide, with the artist's signature, and tastefully framed.

Do you want them?

Then send us two yearly subscriptions, and sign a contract to get us eight more in the next four months or sooner.

NEW ADDRESS?
