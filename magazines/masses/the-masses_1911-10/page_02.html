Jack London says:

Find herewith $1.00, for which send me "THE PROPHET AND THE Ass." You are certainly making a noise like a live wire. We can't have too many buzz-plows like "THE PROPHET AND THE Ass" turning up the sodden soil of men's minds. Keep it up. Yours for the Revolution,

The country needs just such that food as you are handing out."—(Prof.) Kendrick P. Shedd. George R. Kirkpatrick writes: "I like it—I am glad you have my dollar. I want the other 11 numbers."
