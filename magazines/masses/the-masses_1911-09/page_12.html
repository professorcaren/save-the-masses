Do you know the greatest misfortune which could befall the party to-day? It would be the accession to our camp of some great popular political leader."

"What! Aren't you a Socialist?"

They bring the pure and simple unionist in touch with a man like you who can tell him the power that lies in his vote, and they give both of you experience in running large affairs.
