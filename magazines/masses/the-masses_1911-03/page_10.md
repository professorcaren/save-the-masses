It is possible that in the future the private merchants will try to counteract the Contract System by forming an organization of their own. But the middle class move slowly, and it is safe to predict that they will not think of organization until it is too late. By the time they are ready the workingmen will have tasted of the sweets of solidarity in buying, and they will stand by their own co-operative organization. They will not hesitate then to take their savings from their banks and invest them in their own enterprises. The experience derived from the operation of the Contract System will prove a powerful educative factor in co-operation.
