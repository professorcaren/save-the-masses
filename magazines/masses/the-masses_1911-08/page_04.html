CHARLES A. HINTEN

From Painting by Charles A. Winter

THE DESTROYER

VER all the world today lurks the war menace. Profound and thorough as our civilization appears it is no more substantial than a cloud shape because at any moment it may be swept to tatters. This end is not impossible nor is it even improbable for though we take no thought of the World War yet the Destroyer lurks always at our gates. The next great international capitalistic dispute may involve all peoples in the most devastating and blighting conflict of history: a conflict in which society will return to an incoherent jumble. The Destroyer waits for the hour when he may hack down all our dreams of better years. And only Socialism can destroy the Destroyer.
