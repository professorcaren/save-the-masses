## Socialists should at least look into The International

This is a liberal magazine of the character of "The New Age" in England, frankly socialist but not an organ of the Party. It is discursive, yet vital.

Among the contributors to THE INTERNATIONAL are:

John Galsworthy, John Butler Yeats, Edwin Markham, Reginald Wright Kauffman, Richard Le Gallienne, James Oppenheim, George Sylvester Viereck, Michael Monahan, Hudson Maxim, Wm. J. Ghent, George Allan England.

Among those whose work (previously unpublished in English) appears in translation are: Brieux, Wedekind, France, Bjornson, Schnitzler, Strindberg, Artzibatcheff, Andreyev, Echegaray, etc.

The International is notable for its mechanical perfection: printing, paper decorations and clear type.
