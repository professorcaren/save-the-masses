The Speaking Booth

A mirror for your voice. Rehearse anything you’ll deliver in the room: your opening introduction, a single argument, or a whole speech. The Booth reads back your pace, your pauses, and every place you reached for an “um.” Nothing leaves this page. No upload, no sign-in. Say it, fix one thing, say it again.

Optional: paste what you plan to say (or use the “written text only” button below)
0:00

The first time you use the Booth it downloads a small speech model (about 75 MB, then cached). After that it’s instant and works offline.

The Booth hears only what a microphone can: pace, pauses, and filler. It says nothing about eye contact, your stance, your card discipline, or whether the far seat could hear you. Those stay the room’s job. Bring your watchers for the rest.
